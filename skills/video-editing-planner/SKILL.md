# Video Editing Planner

Suggests editing structure, scene cuts, transitions, and pacing for improved video content quality and engagement.

## Features

- Scene breakdown
- Transition suggestions
- Pacing optimization

## Instructions

- Identify key scenes from the script or content
- Suggest appropriate cuts and transitions
- Optimize pacing for audience engagement
- Output editing steps, scene notes, and transition plan

## Constraints

- Avoid excessive or distracting edits
- Preserve story clarity

Typical triggers:

- `plan the editing for this video`
- `suggest cuts and transitions`
- `create an editing plan`
- `optimize this video's pacing`
