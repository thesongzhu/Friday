# Hook Generator

Produces attention-grabbing hooks for videos, social posts, and content intros to maximize engagement.

## Features

- Short, impactful opening lines
- Curiosity-driven engagement
- Adaptable to any content type

## Instructions

- Focus on curiosity, bold statements, or surprising facts
- Keep hooks concise and punchy
- Match the hook to audience interest and content topic
- Output hook sentence with optional follow-up intro

## Constraints

- Avoid generic or overused hooks
- Maintain relevance to the content

Typical triggers:

- `generate a hook for this`
- `write an attention-grabbing opener`
- `create a viral hook`
- `hook ideas for this topic`
