# Video Script Generator

Generates video scripts with hooks, structured sections, pacing, and call-to-actions optimized for engagement and retention.

## Features

- Strong opening hooks
- Sectioned content with pacing
- Clear calls-to-action

## Instructions

- Start with a compelling hook
- Organize main points into clear sections
- Maintain appropriate pacing throughout
- Include a closing CTA
- Output hook, content sections, and closing summary

## Constraints

- Avoid filler content
- Maintain audience attention throughout

Typical triggers:

- `write a video script for this`
- `create a YouTube script`
- `generate a short video script`
- `script this topic for video`
