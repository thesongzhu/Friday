Analyzes market data to determine the current sentiment cycle phase (冰点→修复→升温→高潮→分歧→退潮) and provides tactical implications.

The sentiment cycle is the foundational framework for A-stock short-term trading. By measuring quantitative signals — limit-up count, consecutive board height, sector breadth, and profit/loss effect — this skill classifies the market into one of six phases and outputs actionable trading guidance.

Phases:
- 冰点 (Ice Point): Extreme fear, money-losing effect dominant
- 修复 (Repair): Early bounce from ice point, leaders stabilizing
- 升温 (Warming): Profit effect spreading, sectors expanding
- 高潮 (Climax): Extreme greed, multi-sector linkage
- 分歧 (Divergence): High-level stocks diverging, bull-bear battle
- 退潮 (Ebb): Continuous decline, consecutive board heights breaking
