skillKey: trade-leader-scanner
name: 龙头股扫描器
author: friday-trade
---

Identifies current market leader stocks (人气龙头) and their status by scoring candidates on five criteria: 身位规则 (position/consecutive limit-ups), 时间规则 (limit-up timing), 内核规则 (theme alignment), 抗揍度 (resilience through divergence), and 人气度 (popularity/volume).

Takes limit-up pool data and outputs a ranked list of leader candidates with rotation signals.
